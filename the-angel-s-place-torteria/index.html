The menu

Pick your plate, then pick your meat. Everything below is word for word from the menu board on the side of the truck.

Torta the namesake

Mexican sandwich with choice of meat, mayo, ham, melted cheese, beans, fresh lettuce, tomato, onion, and avocado, with jalapeños on the side.

Cubana go big

A bigger version of the "Torta." Includes beans, mayo, 4 types of meats, hot links, egg, ham, melted cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, and avocado.

Tacos

Corn tortilla tacos with your choice of meat, fresh onion, cilantro, and salsas on the side.

Taco Meal

3 corn tortilla tacos, choice of meat, fresh onion, cilantro, and served with a side of rice and beans; salsas on the side.

Sopes / Gorditas

Handmade fried masa, with beans, choice of meat, lettuce, onion, cilantro, tomato, queso fresco, and sour cream.

Burrito cheesy special

Flour tortilla burrito, filled with beans, rice, choice of meat, mozzarella cheese, onion, cilantro, and avocado.

Cheesy Burrito Special: melted white queso over burrito.

Quesadilla Meal

Flour tortilla filled with choice of meat and melted mozzarella cheese. Meal: side of rice, beans, and veggies.
