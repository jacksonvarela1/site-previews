  • Mexican Street Taco Orden de 5

    Soft corn tortillas with seasoned meat, fresh cilantro and chopped grilled onions. Lime and salsa on the side.

  • Rolling Tacos Or a single

    Rolled tacos filled with meat and melted cheese, with guacamole and topped with sour cream.

  • Burrito

    Flour tortilla with rice, beans, meat, onions, grilled cheese, lettuce and guacamole.

  • Burrito Bowl

    Rice, beans, choice of meat, lettuce, pico de gallo, guacamole, cream and salsa.

  • Enchiladas

    Corn tortillas with choice of meat, green salsa, sour cream, lettuce and pico de gallo. Rice and beans on the side.

  • Quesadilla

    Grilled flour tortilla with melted cheese and choice of meat, plus lettuce, pico de gallo, guacamole and sour cream.

  • Mulita

    Two corn tortillas with melted cheese and your choice of meat, with onion, cilantro and salsa.

  • Torta

    Bread with choice of meat, cheese, onions, lettuce, pico de gallo, guacamole and salsa verde.

  • Arroz y Frijol

    Rice served with pinto beans.

  • Kids' Plate

    A small quesadilla (no meat) or two tacos with the meat of their choice.

  • Single Taco

    One soft corn tortilla with seasoned meat, cilantro and grilled onions.
