When the weather turns, the red chairs and the striped umbrella come out on the sidewalk at 312. Pull up a chair.

Neighbors first

Blessing Bags, collected at the shop.

One December the shop turned into a drop-off point for Blessing Bags. Neighbors could bring gloves, socks and hygiene items by the shop, and anyone who wanted to give money could leave it and the shop would do the shopping.

That is the kind of barber shop this is. And the sign-off on the shop's posts says the rest: "Have a blessed day."
