What a $5K lite renovation looks like in a Rogers fourplex

1:22, silent with on-screen captions. Posted Oct 15, 2026.

A walk through a Rogers fourplex unit after a light renovation in the $5,000 to $7,000 range: new light fixtures, paint, appliances and granite counters, an added washer and dryer, and a new vanity, toilet, tub and tile.

Watch with sound on instagram.com (opens in a new tab)

Transcript

We’re here at [a fourplex] here in Rogers. Just wanted to walk y’all through a little bit of what the renovation looked like on this process. For this one, we were able to keep it kind of within that $5,000 to $7,000 range. New light fixtures was a big portion of it all the way throughout the apartment, including in the bathroom and in the bedrooms. All new paint, freshen the place up. In the kitchen, we did all new appliances, new kitchen sink, brand new granite countertops. That’s always a big piece for us. These cabinets were already in good shape and existing, and so we ended up keeping these, just adding cabinet pulls. They ended up keeping the floors on this one just because they were in good shape.

Nobody really wants to have to go to a laundromat or use laundry facilities. Even though this is not the most appealing location for a washer dryer, this was one of the only spots that we could get it added. The tenant, they no longer have to go to a laundromat and spend their money to do so. It’s all right here for them. This is really something that I have continued to prioritize in every unit that I have. If it doesn’t have washer dryer, I can try to add that. It can be pretty simple using the right HVAC guy, a good plumber that can get it tied into the existing plumbing. I have a few electrical items and you’re good to go.

I’ll walk you through the bathroom now. Added a new bathroom vanity, new countertops, new plumbing fixtures, brand new toilet, new tub. I always prefer to go tile, and so this is a piece that we do on most units. It’s gonna hold up a lot longer than a shower surround. Both of these bedrooms also in the past did not have fans in them. And then yeah, just really kind of freshened up each of the closets, put new handles and fixtures on those. This is what a light renovation looks like, but also a value add to a new tenant that’ll move in.
