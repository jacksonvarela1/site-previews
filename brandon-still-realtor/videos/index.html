“A higher standard”

For sellers, 0:21, his own voice

Brandon, Instagram reel, Sep 1, 2026. See the full reel on Instagram (opens in a new tab) (instagram.com)

Read the transcript

Builders are even offering some big incentives and so there’s that competition between sellers. And it requires sellers to really prepare. Buyers, if they come into a home that’s dirty, if they come into a home that’s been really worn, paint is chipping and paint is peeling, then oftentimes you could lose those opportunities on buyers. They’re just looking for a higher standard on what they want to purchase.

“Catch as many buyers as possible”

For sellers, 0:19, his own voice

Brandon, Instagram reel, Sep 1, 2026. See the full reel on Instagram (opens in a new tab) (instagram.com)

Read the transcript

If you’re a seller thinking about, how can I get my home prepared and ready to be able to catch as many buyers as possible. Buyers right now and expectations are a lot higher on what they expect to get out of a negotiation. You’re seeing more than ever buyers willing to walk away from opportunities because they’re not competing as much. And a lot of that is driven by increases in inventory.

“First impressions start outside”

For investors, 0:19, his own voice, captions on screen

Brandon, Instagram reel, Aug 6, 2026. See the full reel on Instagram (opens in a new tab) (instagram.com)

Read the transcript

One of the first things we focused on was the exterior. The parking lot in general was one that easily flooded, tenants were having a bad experience with it, paving the parking lot, striping it, creating a little bit better experience when they’re coming in and out of their place. New front doors, new posts, got a bigger dumpster. I know that doesn’t sound like much, but when you’re living in it, you like to have a clean place.
