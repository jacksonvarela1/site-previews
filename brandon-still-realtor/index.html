Three Fayetteville areas Brandon is watching for appreciation

1:04 on Instagram. Posted Oct 20, 2026. His picks are his opinion as of that date, not a forecast.

Clabber Creek off Mount Comfort, the foothills of Mount Sequoyah, and the streets around Washington Regional near Drake Farms.

Read the transcript

There’s a ton of great spots in Fayetteville. For me, one of those places that comes to mind is Clabber Creek just off Mount Comfort. You’ve got a lot of great new developments that are happening nearby. [...] Ease of access to 49. You miss a lot of that Weddington traffic, kind of the opposite end of the spectrum.

Mount Sequoyah, you can find a really good mix of older, established homes, great architecture, high price points, and more luxury stuff. At the foothills of that, you can find some really great opportunities. That’s a great area that a lot of people want to be.

And the last one for me, really anything in the vicinity of Washington Regional. You know, if you’ve driven just south of Washington Regional, you’ll see a lot of starter homes, but I think there’s a really good mix of starter homes, older existing homes in kind of the northwest corner. And then obviously a lot of shops, Drake Farms, where you’re going to see a place like Onyx Coffee that just announced they’re going to open up there near Whole Foods as well. That’s an area where I know we’ll continue to see a lot of appreciation because of the demand at Washington Regional. I’m very bullish on anything around Washington Regional and its ability for resale as well.

Renovating 1980s apartment units, one unit at a time

0:42. Posted Aug 6, 2026

The first round of work on 1980s apartment units bought in December 2025, and the plan to hold them for the long term.

Read the transcript

We purchased these units last December [price left out] right here in the central part of Northwest Arkansas. One of the first things we focused on was the exterior. The parking lot in general was one that easily flooded, tenants were having a bad experience with it, paving the parking lot, striping it, creating a little bit better experience when they’re coming in and out of their place. New front doors, new posts, got a bigger dumpster. I know that doesn’t sound like much, but when you’re living in it, you like to have a clean place.

We’re looking to kind of keep these between $10,000 to $15,000 when we’re doing renovations. It’s a great spot, it’s a quiet neighborhood, and these are the kind of properties that we really go after. The plan here is to hold long-term, to continue to renovate, to increase the tenant experience, full overhauls, new vinyl planks, new appliances, new countertops, hold long-term and reap the benefits of cash flow.

What a $5K lite renovation looks like in a Rogers fourplex

1:22. Posted Oct 15, 2026

Light fixtures, paint, appliances, granite counters and an added washer and dryer, kept to about $5,000 to $7,000.

Read the transcript

We’re here at [a fourplex] here in Rogers. Just wanted to walk y’all through a little bit of what the renovation looked like on this process. For this one, we were able to keep it kind of within that $5,000 to $7,000 range. New light fixtures was a big portion of it all the way throughout the apartment, including in the bathroom and in the bedrooms. All new paint, freshen the place up. In the kitchen, we did all new appliances, new kitchen sink, brand new granite countertops. That’s always a big piece for us. These cabinets were already in good shape and existing, and so we ended up keeping these, just adding cabinet pulls. They ended up keeping the floors on this one just because they were in good shape.

Nobody really wants to have to go to a laundromat or use laundry facilities. Even though this is not the most appealing location for a washer dryer, this was one of the only spots that we could get it added. The tenant, they no longer have to go to a laundromat and spend their money to do so. It’s all right here for them. This is really something that I have continued to prioritize in every unit that I have. If it doesn’t have washer dryer, I can try to add that. It can be pretty simple using the right HVAC guy, a good plumber that can get it tied into the existing plumbing. I have a few electrical items and you’re good to go.

I’ll walk you through the bathroom now. Added a new bathroom vanity, new countertops, new plumbing fixtures, brand new toilet, new tub. I always prefer to go tile, and so this is a piece that we do on most units. It’s gonna hold up a lot longer than a shower surround. Both of these bedrooms also in the past did not have fans in them. And then yeah, just really kind of freshened up each of the closets, put new handles and fixtures on those. This is what a light renovation looks like, but also a value add to a new tenant that’ll move in.
