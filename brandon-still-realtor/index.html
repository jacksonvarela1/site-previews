Where Brandon works

Seven towns, each with its own pull.

Benton County

Bentonville

Walmart's hometown, with Crystal Bridges, The Momentary and some 70 miles of mountain-bike trail starting near the downtown square.

Benton County

Rogers

Brick-street downtown, Pinnacle Hills and the Walmart AMP, with Beaver Lake and Hobbs State Park a short drive away. Home base for Brandon's office.

Washington County

Springdale

A downtown arts district on the Razorback Greenway, Naturals baseball, the Jones Center and the Fitzgerald Mountain trails.

Washington County

Fayetteville

The Razorbacks, the square and Dickson Street. It added more residents in 2024–25 than any other city in the state.

Benton County

Bella Vista

Seven lakes, seven golf courses and more than 100 miles of singletrack. Popular with retirees and outdoor families alike.

Benton County

Centerton

A former orchard town just west of Bentonville and Arkansas's fastest-growing city three years running.

Washington County

Tontitown

Settled by Italian immigrants in 1898 and home to the Grape Festival. About ten minutes from Springdale and Fayetteville on Hwy 412.
