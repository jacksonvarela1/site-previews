“A higher standard”

Brandon, Instagram reel, Sep 1, 2026. See the full reel on Instagram (instagram.com)

Builders are even offering some big incentives and so there’s that competition between sellers. And it requires sellers to really prepare. Buyers, if they come into a home that’s dirty, if they come into a home that’s been really worn, paint is chipping and paint is peeling, then oftentimes you could lose those opportunities on buyers. They’re just looking for a higher standard on what they want to purchase.

“First impressions start outside”

For investors, 0:19, his own voice, captions on screen

One of the first things we focused on was the exterior. New front doors, new posts, got a bigger dumpster. I know that doesn’t sound like much, but when you’re living in it, you like to have a clean place.
