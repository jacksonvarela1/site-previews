Living in Farmington, AR

What it’s like to live in Farmington

Farmington sits just west of Fayetteville along Highway 62, which is now five lanes through town. It’s only a few miles from the University of Arkansas, and growth along the 62 corridor has brought a steady run of new subdivisions to what used to be a small farming town.

Farmington fits buyers who work in Fayetteville and want a newer home and a smaller-town feel without a long drive.

Newer homes

New subdivisions keep coming as Fayetteville growth spreads west.

Small-town feel

Its own school district, city parks and community events like FreedomFest.

Location

A few minutes to west Fayetteville and the University of Arkansas, with I-49 a short drive east, depending on traffic.

Farmington neighborhoods

Where you buy in Farmington changes your price, lot size, commute and school. Here’s a quick guide:

  • Newer subdivisions: many homes for sale here are in newer neighborhoods. HOAs are common, so read the covenants before you buy, especially if you might rent it out later.
  • Established areas: older homes near the center of town and along Highway 170.
  • Rural edges: homes on acreage toward Prairie Grove and south of town, for buyers who want more room.

Farmington schools

Most Farmington addresses are served by Farmington Public Schools, but parts of town fall in the Fayetteville or Prairie Grove districts. School boundaries can change and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect, so always confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy. I’m happy to help you check.

Buying an investment property in Farmington

Farmington’s location next to Fayetteville and the University of Arkansas is the main draw for renters, and newer homes can mean lower maintenance. Check HOA rules on leasing before you buy. Start with my guide to investing in Northwest Arkansas.

Is Farmington a good place to live?

Yes, for many buyers. You get newer homes, its own school district and a short drive to Fayetteville. The trade-off is Highway 62 traffic at rush hour as the area keeps growing.

Is Farmington a good place to buy a rental property?

Its location next to Fayetteville and the university draws renters, and newer homes mean lower maintenance. Check HOA leasing rules before you buy.

Should I buy in Farmington or Fayetteville?

Farmington usually means a newer home and a smaller-town feel. Fayetteville gives you more variety, more price points and walkable neighborhoods closer to everything. Tell me your budget and I’ll show you both.

What school district is Farmington in?

Most of Farmington is in Farmington Public Schools, with some addresses in the Fayetteville or Prairie Grove districts. Always confirm the district for a specific address before you buy.
