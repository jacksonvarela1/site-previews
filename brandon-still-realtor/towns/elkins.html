Living in Elkins

Elkins sits east of Fayetteville along Highway 16, on the west bank of the White River. It is the gateway to the Pig Trail, the scenic drive into the Ozarks.

Homes in town sit alongside rural properties on acreage in the surrounding hills, and land is a big part of what sells here.

Space and acreage
Homes in town plus rural properties on acreage in the surrounding hills.
Outdoors
The White River for fishing and canoeing, with the Ozark National Forest nearby.
Location
East of Fayetteville on Highway 16. Rural properties farther out mean a longer drive.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Elkins schools

Most Elkins addresses are served by the Elkins School District, but some fall in the Fayetteville district, and rural addresses farther out can be in other districts such as Huntsville.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Elkins

Elkins is a smaller rental market than Fayetteville, so run the numbers carefully against what similar homes rent for nearby. For rural properties, check the well, septic and road access before you buy.

Elkins real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Elkins, AR?

Washington County’s median sale price was $350,000 in September 2026 (snapshot above). His one Elkins sale on that record was in the $200K to $299K range. Rural properties with acreage vary widely, so prices in Elkins depend a lot on the land. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

What is Elkins like?

It suits a quieter, rural setting with the White River close by. The trade-off is a longer drive to shopping, jobs and I-49.

Should I buy in Elkins or Fayetteville?

Elkins usually means more land and a rural setting. Fayetteville gives you more variety and a shorter drive to everything.

Can I buy land in Elkins?

Yes. Check water, septic, road access and any deed restrictions before you buy, and Brandon can help you look into each one.
