Living in Pea Ridge

Pea Ridge sits northeast of Rogers and Bentonville, close to the Missouri line. It has a small-town feel, its own school district, and newer neighborhoods that have grown quickly as buyers look for more house and land for the money within driving distance of Rogers and Bentonville.

The town is home to Pea Ridge National Military Park, a preserved Civil War battlefield with a driving tour and hiking trails.

Small-town feel
Its own schools and local events.
More for the money
Newer homes and bigger lots than in Bentonville and Rogers, for many buyers.
History and outdoors
Pea Ridge National Military Park and quick access to Beaver Lake. About 20 minutes to Rogers and 25 to Bentonville, depending on traffic.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Pea Ridge neighborhoods

Where you buy in Pea Ridge changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

Newer subdivisions

Newer homes at lower prices than Bentonville or Rogers.

In town

Older homes close to schools and local shops.

Acreage and rural homes

Room for a shop, animals or just space. Check wells, septic and road access.

Pea Ridge schools

Most Pea Ridge addresses are served by the Pea Ridge School District.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Pea Ridge

Newer homes can make Pea Ridge worth a look for a rental. Check HOA rules on leasing and run rent comps carefully, since the rental pool is smaller than in the bigger cities.

Pea Ridge real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Pea Ridge, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). His one Pea Ridge sale on that record was in the $400K to $499K range. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

What is Pea Ridge like?

It is a small town with its own schools and a slower pace. The trade-off is the drive: Rogers and Bentonville are about 20 to 25 minutes away, and there is less shopping and dining in town.

How far is Pea Ridge from Bentonville and Rogers?

About 20 minutes to Rogers and 25 to Bentonville off-peak. Rush hour can add 10 minutes or more.

Can I buy acreage near Pea Ridge?

Yes. There is a good mix of acreage and rural homes around Pea Ridge. Before you buy, check the well and septic, road access, flood zones and any restrictions on the land.
