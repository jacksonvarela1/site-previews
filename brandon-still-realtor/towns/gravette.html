What it’s like to live in Gravette

Gravette is a small town in western Benton County, west of Bella Vista and north of Siloam Springs. Buyers come for a quieter pace, room to spread out and lower prices than the core of NWA, within driving distance of Bentonville.

Quiet pace
Small-town living with its own schools.
Land and space
More acreage and larger lots than neighborhoods closer to Bentonville.
Value
Often more home and land for the money. Bentonville is about 25 to 30 minutes away.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Gravette neighborhoods

Where you buy in Gravette changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • In town

    Older homes close to schools and local shops, at some of the lower prices in Benton County.

  • Newer subdivisions

    Newer homes for less than closer to Bentonville.

  • Acreage and rural homes

    Room to spread out. Check wells, septic and road access.

Gravette schools

Most Gravette addresses are served by the Gravette School District.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab).

Buying an investment property in Gravette

Lower prices can help the numbers on a rental, but the renter pool is smaller. Run rent comps carefully and plan for longer vacancy than in the bigger cities.

Gravette real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Gravette, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon has no closed sale in Gravette on his Zillow record yet, so ask him for recent sales near the homes you like. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Gravette a good place to live?

It is a small town with room to spread out. The trade-offs are the drive to Bentonville and fewer shops and restaurants in town.

How far is Gravette from Bentonville?

About 25 to 30 minutes, depending on where you are in town and traffic.

Can I buy land or acreage near Gravette?

Yes. Gravette is one of the better places in Benton County to find acreage. Check the well and septic, road access, flood zones and any restrictions on the land before you buy.
