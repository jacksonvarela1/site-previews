Living in Cave Springs

Cave Springs is a small town in the middle of Northwest Arkansas, between Bentonville, Rogers, Lowell and Springdale. It is known for larger lots and newer homes, and for the spring-fed cave and lake the town is named for.

It feels quieter than the bigger cities around it, and it sits close to the airport.

Larger lots
More room per home than most nearby towns, plus custom homes and acreage.
Central location
About 10 to 15 minutes to Bentonville, Rogers and XNA, depending on traffic.
Newer homes
Active subdivisions as the town grows.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Cave Springs neighborhoods

Where you buy in Cave Springs changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

Newer subdivisions

Larger homes on bigger lots. HOAs are common, so read the covenants, especially if the home could be a rental later.

Custom homes and acreage

Room to spread out, often at higher price points.

New construction

Buffington Homes, now Buffington Homes by Toll Brothers, has built in Otter Creek and Allen’s Mill, and local custom builders handle many of the larger homes.

Cave Springs schools

Cave Springs addresses can fall in more than one school district.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Buying an investment property in Cave Springs

Newer homes help keep repair costs down, but higher prices make cash flow harder. Brandon’s investing notes describe Cave Springs as mostly a higher-end, owner-occupant market, so check HOA leasing rules first and run the numbers carefully.

Cave Springs real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Cave Springs, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 9 Cave Springs sales on his Zillow record run from the $400K to $499K range to the $1M and up range, and the most common were $400K to $499K and $500K to $749K, 4 each. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

What is Cave Springs like?

You get space, a newer home and a central location. The trade-offs are fewer shops and restaurants in town, and addresses that fall in different school districts, so check the specific address.

Why do buyers choose Cave Springs?

Bigger lots, newer homes and a location central to Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale and the airport.

Is Cave Springs a good place to buy a rental property?

Newer homes help keep repair costs down, but higher prices make cash flow harder. Check HOA leasing rules first.
