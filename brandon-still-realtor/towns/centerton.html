Living in Centerton

Centerton sits just west of Bentonville and has grown quickly, with many newer neighborhoods and new construction. Buyers choose it for the newer homes and its location close to Bentonville’s jobs, trails and downtown.

Shops and services keep following the new rooftops, though there are fewer restaurants in town for now.

Newer homes
Whole neighborhoods built in the last 10 years.
Close to Bentonville
About 10 minutes to Bentonville, 15 to XNA and 20 to Rogers, depending on traffic.
Room to grow
New neighborhoods, shops and services still filling in.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Centerton neighborhoods

Where you buy in Centerton changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

Newer subdivisions

Many homes built in the last 10 years, with HOAs common.

Near the Bentonville line

The shortest drives to Bentonville’s jobs and trails.

New construction

Active subdivisions from national and local builders. Schuber Mitchell Homes, Lennar and D.R. Horton are the builders Brandon sees most here.

Centerton schools

Much of Centerton is served by Bentonville Public Schools.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Centerton

Newer homes mean lower maintenance, and demand from Bentonville keeps renters coming. The catches are HOA leasing rules and lots of similar homes competing for the same tenants, so price the rent carefully. Brandon’s investing notes put newer Centerton homes closer to an appreciation play than a cash-flow one.

Centerton real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Centerton, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 6 Centerton sales on his Zillow record run from the under $200K range to the $400K to $499K range, and the most common were $200K to $299K and $300K to $399K, 2 each. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Should I buy in Centerton or Bentonville?

Centerton usually gets you a newer, bigger home for the money. Bentonville gets you closer to downtown, the trails and the Walmart Home Office. If the commute is to Bentonville, Centerton is only about 10 minutes away.

Who is building new homes in Centerton?

Schuber Mitchell Homes, Lennar and D.R. Horton are the names you’ll see most, along with smaller local builders. New construction here usually costs less than a comparable new home in Bentonville, but prices vary by builder and subdivision, so ask Brandon what is selling now and ask about incentives before you sign.

What is Centerton like?

You get newer homes and a short drive to Bentonville’s jobs and trails. The trade-offs are traffic on the main roads as the town grows, and fewer restaurants and shops in town for now.
