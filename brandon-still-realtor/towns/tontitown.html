What it’s like to live in Tontitown

Tontitown sits along Highway 412 just west of Springdale. Its history goes back to its founding in the 1890s, and the Tontitown Grape Festival celebrates it every summer.

Today it is one of the faster-growing parts of NWA, with many newer neighborhoods and new construction.

Newer homes
Many newer subdivisions and new construction.
Heritage
Local wineries and the annual Grape Festival.
Location
About 10 to 15 minutes to Springdale, 20 to XNA and 25 to Fayetteville, depending on traffic.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Tontitown neighborhoods

Where you buy in Tontitown changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • Newer subdivisions

    Many homes built in the last 10 years. HOAs are common, so read the covenants, especially if the home could be a rental later.

  • Established areas

    Older homes and bigger lots near the heart of town.

  • New construction

    Active subdivisions along the Highway 412 corridor. Schuber Mitchell Homes and D.R. Horton are the most active builders Brandon sees here.

Tontitown schools

Many Tontitown addresses are served by Springdale Public Schools.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Tontitown

Newer homes can mean lower maintenance for a rental, and demand from Springdale employers is steady. Check HOA rules on leasing before you buy, and keep Brandon’s point about new-build equity in mind when you run resale numbers.

Tontitown real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Tontitown, AR?

Washington County’s median sale price was $350,000 in September 2026 (snapshot above). His two Tontitown sales on that record were in the $400K to $499K and $750K to $999K ranges. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Tontitown a good place to live?

You get newer homes, a small-town feel and quick access to Springdale. The trade-off is traffic on Highway 412 at rush hour.

Should I buy in Tontitown or Springdale?

Tontitown usually means a newer home and a quieter neighborhood. Springdale gives you more variety and price points, and a shorter drive to everything.

Who is building new homes in Tontitown?

Several builders are active along the Highway 412 corridor; Schuber Mitchell Homes and D.R. Horton are the most active. Builder pricing changes often, so ask Brandon what is current, and ask about incentives before you sign.
