What it’s like to live in Rogers

Rogers has a little of everything: a historic downtown with local shops and restaurants, Pinnacle Hills Promenade for shopping and dining, and the Walmart AMP for outdoor concerts. Lake Atalanta and the Razorback Greenway put trails right in town, and Beaver Lake is a short drive east.

Brandon works out of Collier & Associates, Rogers Branch, at 5100 W Park Ave Ste A122. The office line is 479.633.7411.

Rogers neighborhoods

Where you buy in Rogers changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • Downtown Rogers

    Older homes with character, walkable to shops and restaurants.

  • Pinnacle Hills area

    Newer, higher-end homes close to the Promenade, the Walmart AMP and I-49.

  • Central Rogers

    Established neighborhoods with older homes on bigger lots, often the best value in town.

  • Southwest Rogers and new construction

    The most new building, toward Cave Springs, from production homes to large custom builds.

Rogers schools

Most of Rogers is served by Rogers Public Schools. Parts of west Rogers may fall in Bentonville’s district.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab).

Rogers real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Rogers, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 23 Rogers sales on his Zillow record run from the $200K to $299K range to the $1M and up range, and the most common was $300K to $399K (7 of 23). For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Rogers a good place to live?

There is something at almost every price point, and the town changes a lot from one side to the other. The trade-offs Brandon names are traffic around Pinnacle and I-49 at rush hour, and some newer areas that feel more suburban than small-town.

Rogers or Bentonville?

They share a border, and the drive between them is about 15 minutes. Rogers usually gives more space and value; Bentonville puts you closer to its downtown and trails. Some west Rogers addresses are in Bentonville’s school district, so check the specific address.

Who is building new homes in Rogers?

Southwest Rogers toward Cave Springs has the most new building. Schuber Mitchell Homes has a new community in the works, Bespoke Homes is based in Rogers, and Buffington Homes is now part of Toll Brothers. Ask about incentives before you sign.
