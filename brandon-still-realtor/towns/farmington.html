What it’s like to live in Farmington

Farmington sits just west of Fayetteville along Highway 62, which is now five lanes through town. It is only a few miles from campus, and growth along the 62 corridor has brought a steady run of new subdivisions to what used to be a small farming town.

It has its own school district, city parks and community events like FreedomFest.

Newer homes
New subdivisions keep coming as Fayetteville growth spreads west.
Small-town feel
City parks and community events like FreedomFest.
Location
A few minutes to west Fayetteville and campus, with I-49 a short drive east, depending on traffic.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Farmington neighborhoods

Where you buy in Farmington changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • Newer subdivisions

    Many of the homes for sale are in newer neighborhoods along the 62 corridor.

  • Established areas

    Older homes near the center of town and along Highway 170.

  • Rural edges

    Homes on acreage toward Prairie Grove and south of town, with more room.

Farmington schools

Most Farmington addresses are served by Farmington Public Schools, with some in the Fayetteville or Prairie Grove districts.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Farmington real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Farmington, AR?

Washington County’s median sale price was $350,000 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 4 Farmington sales on his Zillow record run from the under $200K range to the $500K to $749K range, and the most common was under $200K (2 of 4). For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Farmington a good place to live?

You get newer homes, its own school district and a short drive to Fayetteville. The trade-off is Highway 62 traffic at rush hour as the area keeps growing.

Is Farmington a good place to buy a rental property?

Its location next to Fayetteville and campus draws renters, and newer homes mean lower maintenance. Check HOA leasing rules before you buy.

Farmington or Prairie Grove?

They sit right next to each other on Highway 62. Farmington puts you closer to Fayetteville; Prairie Grove usually means a little more distance and a quieter town.
