What it’s like to live in Springdale

Springdale sits in the middle of Northwest Arkansas, which makes it an easy base for working anywhere in the region. It is home to Tyson Foods’ headquarters, Arvest Ballpark and the Northwest Arkansas Naturals, the Jones Center and a revitalized downtown along Emma Avenue.

The Razorback Greenway runs right through town. Most of Springdale is in Washington County, and part of it reaches into Benton County.

Central location
About 15 minutes to Fayetteville and Rogers, 20 to XNA and 25 to Bentonville, depending on traffic.
Downtown revival
Emma Avenue, the Jones Center and the Greenway through downtown.
Value
Prices below the Bentonville core, for homes and rentals alike.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Springdale neighborhoods

Where you buy in Springdale changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • Downtown and Emma Avenue

    Character homes at some of the friendliest prices in central NWA.

  • East toward Beaver Lake

    Bigger lots and a more rural feel.

  • West Springdale

    Newer subdivisions near Highway 412 and I-49.

  • New construction

    Mostly on the west and north sides of town.

Springdale schools

Most of Springdale is served by Springdale Public Schools. Some parts of town fall in other districts.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Springdale

Brandon’s guide sees Springdale as one of the better places in NWA for cash flow, because prices are lower and rent demand from local employers is strong. Older single-family homes and small multifamily are where most of the numbers work.

Budget for repairs, and inspect the roof, HVAC and plumbing closely on older homes.

Springdale real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Springdale, AR?

Washington County’s median sale price was $350,000 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 27 Springdale sales on his Zillow record run from the under $200K range to the $500K to $749K range, and the most common was $300K to $399K (16 of 27). For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Springdale a good place to live?

You are in the middle of everything without Bentonville prices, and downtown has changed a lot. The trade-offs Brandon names are traffic on Highway 412 and some older parts of town that are still catching up.

How long are the drives from Springdale?

About 15 minutes to Fayetteville and Rogers, 20 to XNA and 25 to Bentonville. Rush hour on I-49 and Highway 412 can add 10 minutes or more.

Who is building new homes in Springdale?

Most new building is on the west and north sides. Lennar, Schuber Mitchell Homes and Baumann & Crosno are the builders Brandon sees most. Ask about incentives before you sign.
