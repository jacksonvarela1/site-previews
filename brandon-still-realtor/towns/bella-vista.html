Living in Bella Vista

Bella Vista sits just north of Bentonville, with wooded hills, several lakes, multiple golf courses and a growing trail system that draws mountain bikers.

It is the place in Benton County for more trees and space while staying close to Bentonville, and the Property Owners Association shapes a lot of what buying here looks like.

Lakes and golf
Several lakes and multiple golf courses spread through the hills.
Trails
A growing trail system, with Bentonville’s trails close by.
The POA
Many homes are in the Bella Vista Property Owners Association, which comes with dues and amenities. Check the current dues and what they cover before you buy.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Bella Vista neighborhoods

Golf course homes

Bella Vista schools

Many Bella Vista addresses are served by Bentonville Public Schools.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Bella Vista

A rental can work here, especially an older home bought at a good price. Bella Vista has lower entry prices than much of Benton County, but factor POA dues into the numbers, and check any rules on rentals before you buy.

Bella Vista real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Bella Vista, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 10 Bella Vista sales on his Zillow record run from the under $200K range to the $500K to $749K range, and the most common were $200K to $299K and $400K to $499K, 3 each. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

What is Bella Vista like?

It is the spot for lakes, golf, trails and quiet with Bentonville close by. The trade-offs are POA dues, hilly lots that can be tricky to build on, and a longer drive to Fayetteville.

What should I know about the Bella Vista POA?

Many homes are in the Property Owners Association. The dues help pay for amenities like the lakes, golf courses and community facilities. Rates and rules change, so check the current dues with the POA for the specific property before you buy.

Are there lots to build on in Bella Vista?

Yes, plenty, but terrain matters. Steep or rocky lots can add a lot to site work and foundation costs. Before you buy, have a builder look at it and check access to utilities.
