What it’s like to live in Lowell

Lowell is a smaller town between Rogers and Springdale, with easy access to I-49 and the Razorback Greenway. It is home to J.B. Hunt’s headquarters, and its central location makes it easy to get anywhere in Northwest Arkansas.

Central location
Rogers and Springdale are about 10 minutes away.
Small-town feel
A quieter town with its own identity, surrounded by the region’s biggest employers.
Trails
The Razorback Greenway toward Rogers and Springdale.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Lowell neighborhoods

Where you buy in Lowell changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • Established neighborhoods

    Older homes on bigger lots, often a good value for a first home or a first rental.

  • Near the Greenway

    Easy trail access to Rogers and Springdale.

  • New construction

    Newer subdivisions on the edges of town, where HOAs are common.

Lowell schools

Buying an investment property in Lowell

Being next to J.B. Hunt and between Rogers and Springdale keeps rent demand steady. The smaller housing stock means fewer deals come up, so be ready to move when one does.

In December 2025 Brandon represented the buyer on a 12-unit apartment building in Lowell that was never listed, bought as a value-add deal and now being renovated, per his own sold page. He reached the owner through direct-to-owner marketing.

Lowell real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Lowell, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). Brandon’s 8 Lowell sales on his Zillow record run from the $200K to $299K range to the $750K to $999K range, and the most common was $300K to $399K (3 of 8). For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Lowell a good place to live?

It is a smaller town that is central to everything: minutes from Rogers, Springdale and I-49, with the Greenway nearby. The trade-off is fewer restaurants and shops in town, though Rogers and Springdale are close.

How does Lowell compare to Rogers or Springdale?

Lowell is smaller and quieter, and usually a little less expensive. You give up some in-town shopping and dining, but Rogers and Springdale are about 10 minutes away.

Is Lowell a good place to buy a rental property?

It can be. Being next to J.B. Hunt and between Rogers and Springdale keeps rent demand steady, but fewer deals come up, so be ready to move when one does.
