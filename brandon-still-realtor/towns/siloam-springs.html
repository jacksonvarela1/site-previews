What it’s like to live in Siloam Springs

Siloam Springs is on the western edge of Northwest Arkansas, right on the Oklahoma line along Highway 412. It is a town in its own right, with a walkable downtown along Sager Creek, John Brown University, its own school district and major local employers.

Springdale, Bentonville and Fayetteville are all within driving distance, and XNA is about 30 minutes away.

Downtown
A walkable historic downtown along Sager Creek with local shops, restaurants and events.
College town
John Brown University brings events, jobs and steady rental demand.
Drive times
About 35 to 40 minutes to Bentonville or Springdale and about 30 to XNA, depending on traffic.

Drive times are Brandon’s estimates and change with traffic.

Siloam Springs neighborhoods

Where you buy in Siloam Springs changes the price, the lot size and the commute. A quick guide:

  • Near downtown

    Older homes with character, close to Sager Creek and local shops.

  • Established neighborhoods

    Mid-range homes on bigger lots.

  • New construction

    Newer subdivisions on the edges of town. Read the covenants before you buy.

Siloam Springs schools

Most Siloam Springs addresses are served by the Siloam Springs School District.

School boundaries change, and some addresses fall in a different district than you’d expect. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district before you buy; Brandon can help you check.

Official district information: the Arkansas Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, on dese.ade.arkansas.gov (opens in a new tab)

Buying an investment property in Siloam Springs

Local employers and John Brown University keep demand steady. Look at both single-family and small multifamily, and run rent comps for the specific part of town.

Homes near JBU can rent well to students and staff. Expect more turnover, and check zoning and occupancy rules before renting by the room.

Siloam Springs real estate FAQ

How much does a home cost in Siloam Springs, AR?

Benton County’s median sale price was $363,600 in September 2026 (snapshot above). His one Siloam Springs sale on that record was in the under $200K range. For a specific street, ask Brandon what sold within a mile. That is the number that matters.

Is Siloam Springs a good place to live?

It is a self-contained town with its own schools, downtown and jobs. The trade-off is the drive: Springdale and Bentonville are about 35 to 40 minutes away.

How long is the drive from Siloam Springs to Bentonville or Springdale?

About 35 to 40 minutes to Bentonville or Springdale, and about 30 minutes to XNA, depending on traffic.

Does it make sense to buy a rental near John Brown University?

Homes near JBU can rent well to students and staff. Expect more turnover with student renters, and check zoning and occupancy rules before renting by the room.
