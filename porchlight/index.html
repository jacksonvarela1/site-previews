Questions

The honest answers.

Do I have to be on camera or film anything?

No. We start with the photos and videos you already have. On Growth we come by once a month to film your work, your food or your space, not you, unless you want to be in it.

Who owns my accounts?

You do. You give us posting access and can take it back any time. Everything we make for you is yours to keep.

What if I do not like a post?

It does not go up. Nothing posts in your name until you approve it, and we redo anything you do not love.

Do you use AI?

Yes, to help draft captions and edits faster. A person checks every post, and you approve it before it goes live. We never buy followers or use bots on your account.

Can I cancel?

Any time, with a text. No contract and no fee. You are never charged after you cancel.
