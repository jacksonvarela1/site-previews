04 / Memorial Day

The Murph

Done in honor of Lt. Michael Murphy, a Navy SEAL who was killed in Afghanistan in 2005 during Operation Red Wings. The workout serves as a reminder of sacrifice, courage, and service to others.

"Come honor the fallen with us and put in hard work alongside the community." As always, it can be scaled for all fitness levels.
