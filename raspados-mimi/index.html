Especialidades

The Best Place For A Snack

Fruit with chamoy and chili, corn in a cup, chilindrinas and the Crazy Pineapple. Every one of these is on the Raspados Mimis menu.

  • Chilindrina

    A large rectangular flour chip coated with cabbage, tomato, avocado, porkskin, cheese, mayo and sour cream.

  • Crazy Pineapple

    Just what it sounds like. A crazy good pineapple with fruit, soda, tamarind candy, chamoy and chili powder.

  • Fruit Salad

    A beautiful cup of fruit with cucumber, mango, pineapple, jicama, watermelon, chili powder, lime and chamoy.

  • Rusa

    Soda, fruit juice, bits of fruit, chili powder, chamoy, lime and a stick of tamarind candy.

  • Mango Twist

    A bowl of spiraled mango with lime, salt and chili powder.

  • Mimis Special Salad

    Fruit cut up spirally in a bowl, then coated with chili powder, chamoy and lime.

  • Corn in a Cup

    Corn in a cup with mayo, cheese, lime and chili powder.

    Small · Medium · Large

  • Fried Banana

  • Bionico

    A cup of mixed fruit with a homemade sweet cream and toppings.

  • Strawberries with Cream

    A cup of strawberries with a homemade sweet cream and toppings.

  • Tamarind Covered Apple

    A juicy apple covered with tamarind, coated with spices and candy.

  • Porkskin in a Cup

    Porkskin with candy, chips and spices in a cup.

  • Nachos
