What’s in the house.

Rock House 205 calls it art, antiques and small goods. Here is what the shop lists on its own invitations and posts.

  1. No. 01

    Art

    Framed art and paintings for the walls at home.

  2. No. 02

    Antiques

    Unique, one-of-a-kind pieces for your home space.

  3. No. 03

    Kitchen wares

    Elegant mango wood and enamel serving pieces, plus platters, bowls and glassware for the table.

  4. No. 04

    Local goods

    Jars, lye soap and Wicked Snacks, made in Little Rock.

  5. No. 05

    Jewelry

    Something small to give, or to keep.

  6. No. 06

    Chocolates

    Handmade chocolates, on the Holiday Open Shop list.

    Holiday linens, unusual styles of menorahs and natural beeswax celebration candles.
